Curriculum: 4 Steps to Master

01

MIDI & Producing

Foundations of Creation

  • DAW workflow optimization
  • VST & sound design
  • Drum/bass rhythm arrangement
  • Song form & aesthetics
02

Recording

Pro Sound Capture

  • Comparison of pro mics (U87ai, etc.)
  • Signal flow (Mic-Pre-DAW)
  • Vocal directing & tuning
  • Instrument miking techniques
03

Mixing

Sculpting Sound

  • Balance, panning, stereo imaging
  • EQ & Dynamics (Compressor)
  • Spatial effects (Reverb/Delay)
  • Analog outboard hybrid mixing
04

Mastering

Completion & Release

  • Loudness standards (LUFS)
  • Album tone & consistency
  • Distribution metadata & ISRC
  • Final monitoring & release practice
