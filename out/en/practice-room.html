Practice Room FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the studio.

Q1

Which areas in Eunpyeong-gu are nearby?

We are located right next to Dongmyeong Girls' High School. It's a 5-minute walk from Yeonsinnae Station, making it highly accessible from Yeonsinnae, Bulgwang, Gusan, Yeokchon, and Eungam.

Q2

Is the practice room available 24 hours?

Yes. Resident members can access their rooms freely 24/7 via individual door lock codes.

Q3

How about parking and public transportation?

You can use the affordable public parking lot in Daejo-dong, a 1-2 minute walk away. Public transit access is easy via Bulgwang Station or Yeonsinnae Station (Lines 3 and 6), and the bus stop "Dongmyeong Girls' High School / Catholic Bulgwang-dong Church" is right in front.
