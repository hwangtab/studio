### 🔥 Sound nerds, gather in Bulgwang-dong's mixing hell! "Bulgwang Mixing Club" opens its doors! 🔥

We invite you to the thrilling party for those who live and breathe mixing: "Bulgwang Mixing Club"!
Enough mixing alone with your head in your hands. Come share know-how, level up together, and enjoy the ride.

### 🎧 This is the real mixing hot spot!

"Bulgwang Mixing Club" is not a stiff study group. It's a friendly exchange where you can enjoy great chicken and give honest feedback on each other's music. Chat with fellow musicians and engineers, spark new inspiration, and meet your next musical companions.

### 📢 This week’s meetup info

* When? Friday, November 7, 2025, 7:00 PM (Friday night means mixing time!)
* Where? Bulgwang Studio NOL, R03 Mixing Room (3F, 84-3 Daejo-dong, Eunpyeong-gu)
* Fee: 15,000 KRW (our soul food: chicken!)
  * Studio NOL users are free, of course.
* Capacity: Only 6 hand-picked members! (If it fills up, we’ll announce with tears in our eyes.)

### ❗ Important notice: Check Mondays for the schedule!

"Bulgwang Mixing Club" happens on a weekly basis, and the exact day and time are announced every Monday. Don’t miss the Monday notice!

Text 0507-1384-3144 with “I’m in!” like you mean it.
