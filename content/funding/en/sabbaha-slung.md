## Sounds dredged up from the swamp

![SLUNG front cover — a body lying arms outstretched on black water, dissolving into white spray; SLUNG and SABBAHA are written in the lower left](/images/funding/sabbaha-slung/album-front-20260928.webp)

SLUNG is Sabbaha's second full-length album: thirteen tracks across two CDs, 98 minutes in all. This is how the band introduces it:

> Sounds dredged up from a chaotic swamp where life and death are mixed together.

Recording, mixing and mastering are finished, and the artwork is nearly done. This campaign pays for pressing the CDs and for the release show.

## Sabbaha

![Sabbaha on stage — guitarist The Slaughter, shrouded in white cloth, stands with his guitar; behind him drummer The Mortician, hooded in black, raises both drumsticks](/images/funding/sabbaha-slung/band-live-20260929.webp)

Sabbaha is a doom metal duo working between Seoul and Suwon, South Korea. The Slaughter (guitar, vocals) and The Mortician (drums) call what they play "pseudo-occult doom drone." The name joins svāhā — the word that closes Buddhist mantras, "let it be done" — to Sabbath, the witches' gathering.

A Sabbaha show opens with a drone at crushing volume: the weight of an absolute power bearing down on people, rendered as sound. Then guitar and drums come in, and the doom metal that follows plays out the suffering of those pinned under the fate that power has fixed.

![Sabbaha live in black and white — The Slaughter, covered head to toe in long hair, plays guitar in front of the drum kit](/images/funding/sabbaha-slung/band-bw-20260929.webp)

Since their first show on the last night of 2023 they have played more than eighty times, and in November they play their first show abroad, in Tokyo. Since 2024 they have co-run CURSEWAVE+LOWFREQUENCY (저주파), a doom and extreme-music series, with the Mexican-born, Seoul-based doom musician Nahua, and this year they put together ABYSS MASS FEST, bringing Korean and Japanese heavy bands onto one stage.

Their 2024 debut THUNDER ROCKS was a one-man record — The Slaughter made it alone and programmed the drums. Drummer The Mortician joined in 2025, and SLUNG is the first full-length the two have recorded together.

## SLUNG

The first CD opens with "Kalpa," named after the Buddhist kalpa — an aeon too long to be counted.

> Living a single instant borrowed from eternity… you sing as arrogantly as if you owned the infinite…

%%funding-audio:sabbaha-kalpa%%

After "Yama," a piece about the god of death, the album turns to the lives of people now. "Debt Shroud" is about a debt turned away by seven banks and nine insurers; "Dopaminethirster" is about a life that thirsts for dopamine.

%%funding-audio:sabbaha-debt-shroud%%

The second CD holds five long instrumentals of around ten minutes each. Their titles take on elitism, a séance and an ossuary, and the closing "Stone" carries a single line: "Whatever flourishes must fall" (盛者必滅).

Some of these songs are being released properly for the first time. "Kalpa" and "Yama" appeared only on a limited single sold at the Patchcode Fair in Mangwon-dong, Seoul, in November 2025, and have never been available online. "Debt Shroud," "Apprentice's Work," "Dopaminethirster" and "Stone" first appeared on THUNDER ROCKS and have been recorded anew by the duo.
